# R26 Local Quickstart

1. Create a disposable PostgreSQL database and apply all forward-only migrations.
2. Seed a synthetic workspace, owner, one project, one contact identity and selected evidence.
3. Prepare a disabled Google or Microsoft email account with narrow capabilities.
4. Admit a trusted synthetic normalized project email, then replay it exactly.
5. Admit an ambiguous variant and confirm no consequential project write.
6. Link selected same-project evidence; refuse a cross-workspace reference.
7. Prepare an exact reply, inspect To/Cc/subject/body/thread/evidence and approve the exact version/hash.
8. Retry admission and approval after restart; confirm one effect and zero delivery.
9. Inspect owner and field projections and verify restricted fields are absent.
10. Revoke locally and confirm queued/new operations fail closed while history remains.

No step opens a provider, mailbox, OAuth flow, webhook or network connection.
