# Goal — ENDVERA virtual secretary kernel

## Objective

Make the founder's virtual-secretary definition executable as a local closed-world contract: answer schedule questions, prepare Google Calendar reads, project changes, calendar events, one-to-ten-person texts and calls while keeping every real external effect gated.

## Release exit

- Seven capabilities exist in one typed catalog.
- Every request binds actor, workspace and idempotency.
- Ten-person broadcast is exact, unique and consent-aware.
- Reads, internal writes and external writes remain distinct.
- Mobile TextAssist visibly explains what the secretary can do.
- Root/mobile tests, typechecks and provider-boundary checks pass.

## Persistent product goal

Continue local product construction without routine founder confirmation. A provider credential, spend, customer data, real communication, OAuth, store action or deployment remains a separate urgent authority gate.
