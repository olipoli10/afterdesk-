# Specification Quality Checklist: R28 Organization Authority Policies

- [x] Product outcome is user- and workflow-oriented.
- [x] Role authorization and workspace policy are explicitly separate.
- [x] Closed action registry and default-deny behavior are explicit.
- [x] Immutable versioning, precedence, ceilings and exact decisions are specified.
- [x] Replay, concurrency, restart and tenancy requirements are measurable.
- [x] Prohibited actions cannot be converted into approvals.
- [x] Field-worker projection minimization is explicit.
- [x] Provider, credential, external-effect and customer-data exclusions are explicit.
- [x] External/provider evidence is not overstated.
