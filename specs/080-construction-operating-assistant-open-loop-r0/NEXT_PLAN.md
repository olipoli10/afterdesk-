# Next admitted plan — Founder-owned invoice-readiness loop

## Goal

Run one short, local, founder-owned use of the integrated one-screen `work finished -> invoice-ready` workflow. Measure whether Olivier can understand what is done, what is missing, who must act and whether the dossier is ready to invoice without reconstructing context manually.

## Inputs

- one fully synthetic dossier or one dossier explicitly owned by Olivier;
- no client/prospect data;
- no provider or external transport;
- the same frozen readiness policy as R0.

## Required observations

1. report completed work conversationally;
2. inspect the missing approval/evidence;
3. add one contradiction and verify that both claims remain visible;
4. resolve the contradiction as an authorized owner;
5. add verified approval and supporting evidence;
6. reach `READY_TO_INVOICE`;
7. reload/restart and confirm identical state;
8. prepare an evidence follow-up and confirm it remains unsent;
9. inspect the field-worker view and confirm financial masking.

## Measures

- active founder minutes;
- manual context restatements;
- corrections requested;
- readiness decision accuracy;
- next-actor comprehension;
- actionability rating;
- whether the founder would use the dossier before invoicing.

## Verdicts

- `FOUNDER_OWNED_INVOICE_READINESS_LOOP_OBSERVED_PASS`
- `REWORK`
- `REJECT`

No positive founder verdict may be created from an automated fixture. Messaging, calendar, voice and accounting providers remain a separate later campaign with explicit sandbox authority.

## Discovery immediately after a local founder PASS

Interview ten Québec construction companies about the same `work finished -> invoice-ready` failure and obtain at least three recent dossier walkthroughs. Continue only if at least five independent respondents describe the same costly failure, three walkthroughs show it in recent evidence, and at least three companies express a concrete willingness to pay for a bounded pilot.
