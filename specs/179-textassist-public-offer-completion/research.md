# Research Decisions

## Decision 1: Keep the additive product architecture

The founder explicitly rejected replacing the full site. TextAssist remains a distinct product entry at `/textassist`, backed by a banner plus durable header and footer links.

## Decision 2: Describe outcomes before integrations

The public offer leads with the contractor's daily loop and authority boundary. Provider names and unobserved connector claims are excluded because they are implementation dependencies, not customer value.

## Decision 3: Do not invent pricing

No willingness-to-pay or pricing validation exists. The page labels pricing as in preparation and directs interest to the existing account path without presenting a fabricated plan.
