# Research

## Decision

Use existing accepted release artifacts as protected inputs and create a new aggregate instead of rewriting historical R36K evidence.

## Rationale

R36K truthfully described its earlier state, but it predates the additive TextAssist route, public account-deletion guidance, mobile visual assets and assistant-experience polish. A new immutable report avoids falsifying historical evidence while removing one now-resolved blocker.

## External blockers

The public account-deletion resource is now present locally. Final brand approval was not resolved by candidate artwork and therefore remains explicit. Public infrastructure, legal approval, real-device evidence, signing, store ownership, monitoring and staffed support remain outside local authority.
