# Contract: Credential-Free Provider Preflight

## Perplexity Search candidate

- Endpoint family `PERPLEXITY_SEARCH`.
- Public business facts and public professional-person facts only.
- People mode only for professional context; private contacts, sensitive traits and background checks refuse before plan creation.
- Region, locale, result count and content are bounded.
- Normalization retains bounded title, direct URL, snippet, optional date and fingerprint.
- A factual claim without an identified supporting source fails verification.

## OpenRouter controller candidate

- Endpoint family `OPENROUTER_CHAT_COMPLETIONS`.
- One closed internal model profile; R37 binds it to an exact current model ID.
- Required: supported parameters, `data_collection=deny`, `zdr=true`, provider/model fallback disabled.
- Strict output contract, bounded ordered synthetic facts, no key/contact coordinate/unbounded business record.
- No R36B artifact is dispatchable.

## Direct-controller control

- Distinct candidate packet and endpoint family.
- Same case, facts, order, locale, schemas, verification and ceilings as OpenRouter.

## Benchmark

- Same case and ceiling fingerprints for every candidate.
- Integer microdollars and citation basis points.
- Unequal inputs/ceilings, unknown fields, unsupported claims, invalid URLs, expired evidence or excess spend refuse.
- Only `NO_PROVIDER_SELECTION` with `SYNTHETIC` evidence is permitted.

## Proposed R37 boundary

- Maximum calls: 12.
- Maximum spend: 5,000,000 microdollars (USD 5.00).
- Secret-reference names only.
- State remains `PREPARED_NOT_AUTHORIZED` until separate authority freezes IDs, prices, privacy, budget and credentials.
