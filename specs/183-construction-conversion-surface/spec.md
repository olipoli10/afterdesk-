# Feature Specification: Construction Conversion Surface

**Status**: Accepted for autonomous implementation

## User Scenarios

### User Story 1 - Understand the product in one page (P1)

A small contractor can understand who ENDVERA is for, what happens after a request and what remains under their control without reading the historical managed-work site.

### User Story 2 - See an honest availability boundary (P1)

The contractor sees that the local product can demonstrate persistent context and prepared actions, while live text, voice and external connectors are not yet activated.

### User Story 3 - Reach the next useful action (P1)

The contractor can create a local account, inspect TextAssist, reach support, privacy and account-deletion guidance, or return to the preserved root site.

## Requirements

- The route MUST support equivalent French and English content.
- It MUST explain the request, context, preparation, authorization and persistent-state loop.
- It MUST explain contacts, calendar, job evidence, follow-ups and prepared communications.
- Human backup and consequential-action approval MUST be explicit.
- CTA, privacy, support and deletion paths MUST be present.
- The root homepage MUST remain unchanged.
- Live providers, validated pricing, publication and customer proof MUST not be claimed.

## Success Criteria

The page exposes at least five operating-loop steps, four outcomes, human backup, honest availability, conversion and trust links in both launch languages.
